Case study

Automated Google Ads audit framework

  • Platform81
  • In production
  • Google Ads · Excel · Deck generation

A framework that runs 100+ checks across a Google Ads account, produces a detailed Excel breakdown, surfaces the findings that matter and populates a templated pitch or review deck from them.

  • 100+checks per audit
  • 2+ hrs → ~20 minto initial output
  • ~90%of proposal audits now use it

Problem

Audits sit at the front of almost every new business conversation, and they are expensive to produce. A proper manual audit means working through the account in detail, check by check, then writing the findings up into something a prospect will actually read.

That took at least a couple of hours of analyst time per audit. Worse, because it was manual, the depth varied with how much time the person had that week — the checks that got skipped under pressure were rarely the unimportant ones.

Approach

Most of an audit is not analysis, it is retrieval and comparison against a known standard. That part is deterministic and should never have depended on someone remembering to look.

So I codified the checklist: every check we would run manually, defined once, run identically every time. The framework produces the full breakdown as an Excel workbook so the detail is auditable, identifies the significant findings from that data, and then writes them into a templated deck ready for a person to sharpen.

Workflow

  1. Google Ads account Structure, settings and performance data
  2. Automated check suite 100+ checks run consistently against every account
  3. Excel output Full audit breakdown, check by check
  4. Key findings The issues worth raising, pulled from the detail
  5. Templated deck Pitch or review deck populated from the findings
  6. Human review Analyst validates, prioritises and shapes the story before it reaches a prospect

Human oversight

The framework produces a first draft, not a finished deliverable. Nothing generated by it goes to a prospect without a person reading it.

An automated check can flag something technically true but not worth raising for that advertiser, so deciding which findings matter stays with the analyst. What the framework changes is that they are choosing from the full set of checks rather than whatever there was time to look at.

Impact

  • Initial audit production went from a couple of hours of manual work to roughly twenty minutes.
  • Around 90% of audits completed as part of proposals now run through the framework.
  • My Head of PPC uses it within his own pitching and new business workflow — adoption well beyond my own accounts.
  • Every audit now covers the same 100+ checks regardless of how busy the week is, so depth no longer varies with workload.

A note on detail. I designed and built this framework at Platform81. The specific audit logic and check definitions are the company's, so they are not described here and no code, client data or account identifiers appear on this page.
