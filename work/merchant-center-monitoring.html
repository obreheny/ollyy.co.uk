Merchant Center disapproval monitoring

  • Platform81
  • In production
  • Merchant Center · MCP · Slack

A daily workflow that tracks product approval status across the highest-risk Shopping accounts and posts a Slack alert when a daily check identifies disapprovals.

  • ~30,000products monitored
  • Dailyscheduled checks
  • Slackalerts on disapproval

Problem

Product disapprovals are one of the quietest ways to lose revenue in paid media. A product gets disapproved, it drops out of Shopping and Performance Max, and nothing announces it. Spend simply moves elsewhere and performance dips for a reason that is not visible in the campaign reporting.

Finding it meant remembering to open Merchant Center and look, on accounts with tens of thousands of products, across a portfolio where some feeds are far more fragile than others. In practice, disapprovals were often found days later while investigating something else.

Approach

This one did not need intelligence, it needed reliability. The check is simple and the value is entirely in it happening every single day without anybody deciding to do it.

So the workflow runs on a schedule against the accounts where feed problems actually occur, retrieves approval and disapproval status, logs it so status can be compared over time, and pushes an alert into Slack when something changes for the worse.

Workflow

  1. Scheduled daily run High-risk Shopping accounts, ~30,000 products
  2. Merchant Center MCP integration Retrieves product approval and disapproval status
  3. Status log Approval status recorded so changes can be compared day to day
  4. Slack alert Triggered when disapprovals appear
  5. Human diagnosis & fix Account manager investigates the cause and resolves it with the client or developer

Human oversight

The workflow monitors and alerts. It does not change feeds, edit product data or attempt to resolve disapprovals on its own.

That split is deliberate: the cause of a disapproval usually sits outside the ad platform, in the feed or on the site, and fixing it means a conversation with the client or their developer. Automating the monitoring removes the part that was failing, which was noticing. The diagnosis stays with a person.

Impact

  • Around 30,000 products across problem accounts are checked every day rather than whenever someone thinks to look.
  • Disapprovals identified by the daily check surface in Slack for investigation.
  • Approval status is logged over time, so recurring feed problems become visible as patterns rather than one-off incidents.

Ownership, stated plainly. I built this monitoring workflow using a shared Merchant Center MCP integration, and I have also contributed code changes to that underlying integration. I did not originally create it. That was my Head of PPC.
