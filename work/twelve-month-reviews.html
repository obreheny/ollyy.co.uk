Case study

MCP-powered 12-month client reviews

  • Platform81
  • In production
  • Google Ads · MCP · Client reporting

A workflow that asks the account manager a short set of strategic questions, retrieves the performance data it needs through a Google Ads MCP integration, and generates the complete annual review deck for them to QA.

  • 2+ hrs → ~2 minactive preparation per review
  • 4 of 4PPC team members using it

Problem

A 12-month performance review is one of the most valuable conversations you have with a client. It is also the one most likely to be rushed, because building the deck takes hours: pulling a year of performance data, comparing it against the previous period, assembling the charts, writing the commentary and formatting it all into something presentable.

Two hours was the floor, and the time went almost entirely into assembly rather than thinking. The strategic part — what the year actually tells us and what we should do differently — got whatever attention was left.

Approach

The data retrieval and the deck assembly are mechanical. The genuinely human input is small but essential: what changed for this client over the year, what the commercial priorities are now, what context sits outside the ad account.

So the workflow front-loads exactly that. It asks the account manager a short series of strategic and contextual questions, then uses a Google Ads MCP integration to pull the live performance data it needs, and generates the deck in the background while they get on with something else.

The account manager's job becomes answering those questions well and QAing the result — which is where their expertise was always worth spending.

Workflow

  1. Strategic questions Account manager answers a short set of contextual prompts — roughly two minutes
  2. Google Ads MCP integration Retrieves the live performance data the review needs
  3. Analysis & commentary Year-on-year performance interpreted against the context provided
  4. Generated review deck Complete 12-month deck produced in the background
  5. Human QA Account manager checks the output and owns it before it reaches the client

Human oversight

Nothing generated here goes in front of a client unchecked. The account manager QAs every deck, and they remain the person accountable for what it says.

Client-facing commentary is where a confidently wrong statement does real damage, and the account manager is the person with the context to catch a claim the data supports but the relationship does not. The workflow removes the assembly work, not the accountability.

Impact

  • Active preparation time dropped from 2+ hours to roughly two minutes, followed by background generation and a final QA pass.
  • Adopted by all four members of the Platform81 PPC team, myself included.

Ownership, stated plainly. I built this workflow. The underlying Google Ads MCP integration it retrieves data through was originally built by my Head of PPC; I run and extend my own version of it and build workflows on top.

No client data or employer code appears on this page. The architecture is described conceptually only.
