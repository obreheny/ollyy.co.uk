Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

This policy covers two things: the ollyy.co.uk website, and the internal tools I operate to manage Google Ads accounts on behalf of my clients.

Who I am

I am Olly Breheny, a freelance paid search consultant based in the United Kingdom. I am the data controller for the processing described in this policy.

You can reach me at ollybreheny@gmail.com with any question about this policy or about data I hold.

This website

ollyy.co.uk is a static site. It has no login, no forms, no comment system and no advertising. I do not set analytics cookies and I do not track visitors across sites.

The site is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Both keep standard technical logs — IP address, timestamp, requested page, browser user-agent — for security and abuse prevention. I do not use those logs to identify individuals, and I have no access to them beyond aggregate traffic figures.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives a request from your browser when a page loads.

Google Ads account management tools

I build and operate internal tools that connect to the Google Ads API to help me manage advertising accounts for clients who have engaged me to do so. This section explains how those tools handle data.

What they access

The tools access Google Ads advertising data for accounts linked to my Google Ads manager account: campaign, ad group, keyword and search term performance, budgets and spend, account structure, and related reporting metrics.

They access these accounts only where the advertiser has authorised me to manage their advertising. They do not access any account outside my manager account.

Why

Solely to carry out the account management work my clients have engaged me for: reporting on performance, monitoring budgets, identifying issues, and making routine optimisation changes.

Who has access

Only me. The tools run locally on my own equipment, authenticate as me, and have no multi-user capability, no hosted interface and no public component. They are not sold, licensed, distributed or made available to anyone else.

What is shared

Nothing. I do not sell, rent or share Google Ads data with third parties. I do not combine one client's data with another's, and I never use one advertiser's data to inform, benchmark or advantage another.

Storage and retention

Reporting data is cached locally on my own equipment so that performance can be compared over time. It is held only as long as it remains useful for that purpose, and is deleted on request or at the end of a client relationship.

Access credentials are stored locally, are never shared, and are never embedded in distributed software.

Google API Services User Data Policy. My use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the account management services described above, is never transferred to others except as required for those services or by law, is never used for advertising purposes, and is never read by humans except with the account holder's consent, for security purposes, or where required by law.

Client data and your rights

If I manage your Google Ads advertising, the data described above relates to your business account rather than to you personally. Where any of it constitutes personal data under UK GDPR, my lawful basis is the legitimate interest of performing the account management services you have engaged me for.

You have the right to ask what data I hold, to request a copy, to have inaccurate data corrected, to request deletion, and to object to processing. Write to me and I will respond within one month.

You can also revoke my access to your Google Ads account at any time through your own Google Ads account settings, which immediately ends all access by these tools.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
